### Kasvatusvinkkejä

::grow-plant{season="Late April to June" temperature="55°F"}

#soil
- **pH Range:** 6.0 to 7.5 (Optimal: 6.5)
- **Type:** Well-drained fertile loam
- **Nitrogen:** 100-120 lbs/acre for grain varieties

#water
Hemp requires approximately **12-15 inches** of water during the vegetative cycle. Avoid saturated soils during the first 3 weeks as young plants are prone to damping off.

#alert
Do not plant seeds deeper than 1.5 inches. Deep sowing is the primary cause of poor stand establishment in fiber hemp.

- **Pre-Plant:** Ensure a firm, fine-textured seedbed for good seed-to-soil contact.
- **Sowing:** Use a grain drill for large scale or a precision seeder for CBD/flower crops.
- **Roll:** Use a cultipacker after seeding to ensure moisture retention.
- **Monitor:** Watch for nitrogen deficiency (yellowing lower leaves) during the rapid growth phase.

#default
Just do it
::

### Sadonkorjuu

::grow-harvest{variety="CBD" labor="High (Manual)" yield="1,800 lbs/acre" thcLimit="0.29%" humidity="55%" window="90-110 Days"}
---
difficulty: 5
---

1. **Flower Selection**: Inspect the top colas for dense floral development and rich terpene aromas.
2. **Timing**: Harvest early in the morning to preserve volatile essential oils.
3. **Hang Drying**: Cut the whole plant at the base and hang upside down in a controlled environment.

#compliance
You could submit a **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** from an accredited lab to the department of agriculture. 

Harvesting without a passing test or past the 30-day window could perhaps one day result in something.

### Vesiviljely

::grow-hydro{system="NFT" stage="Flowering" lightCycle="12/12" co2="1200 ppm" vpd="1.2 kPa" ppfd="800-1000" ecRange="2.2 - 2.6" humidity="45%"}

#nutrients
- **Bloom Base**: 2-8-4 NPK ratio
- **Magnesium Sulfate**: 1g/gallon
- **PK Booster**: Weekly application from Week 3-6
- **Fulvic Acid**: Enhances nutrient uptake in NFT channels

#alert
High EC levels (>2.8) in NFT systems during flowering can lead to rapid salt buildup on root mats. Ensure a 20% reservoir dilution if tip burn is observed.

1. **Daily**: Check for channel clogs or root damming that can cause overflows.
2. **Weekly**: Clean the primary inline filter to maintain flow rate.
3. **Bi-Weekly**: Full reservoir swap to prevent nutrient lockout.
4. **Environment**: Increase airflow to compensate for 1200ppm CO2 enrichment.
::
